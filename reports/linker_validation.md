### 2. Travel-time model and files (stage `times`)

`time = access_origin + network + partial_edge + access_building`, where **access = straight-line snap distance at 15 km/h** (both ends), **partial edge** = the fraction of the snapped edge between its end node and the snap point at that edge's own speed (oneway respected; same-edge origin/target handled directly), **network** = forward Dijkstra (`scipy.sparse.csgraph.dijkstra`, origin → buildings = dispatch direction) on the directed main-SCC arcs, `t_light_s` or `t_truck_s` (truck = light × 0.9 on motorway/trunk/primary, × 0.8 below; network agent). Distance = length of the time-optimal path (+ access), obtained by pointer jumping on the predecessor tree. No turn penalties, no turnout time: **travel-time estimates, not response times.**
