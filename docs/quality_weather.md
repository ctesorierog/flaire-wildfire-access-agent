## 2. Format, units, time zone

- Each station zip holds one XML per month (`CODE_YYYY_M.xml`, ISO-8859-1) + XSD: `<mes><dia Dia="YYYY-M-DD"><hora Hora="HH:MM"><Meteoros><Tag._a_<h>cm>value</…>`. Tag = variable + **sensor height in cm**. Month in `Dia` is not zero-padded. 2018–2022 XSDs list tags only; **2023 XSDs document units**: temperature °C, RH %, precipitation mm, **wind speed / max / sigma m/s**, direction °, irradiance W/m², pressure mb.
- Wind: the collector note (from `abreviaturas.txt`) says km/h; the 2023 XSD says **m/s** and magnitudes agree with m/s (10-min mean median 1.6, p99.9 10.7; highest gust 38 m/s = 137 km/h at C026 on 2018-01-29, a winter storm value; as km/h these would be implausibly calm). Used m/s (= schema unit).
- **Time zone = UTC**, established empirically (the source does not state it):
  1. DST: on all 12 change days (last Sunday of March/October 2018–2023) every station has exactly 144 slots, no duplicated or missing hour (local civil time would give 138/150 slots). Every day in the archive has ≤ 144 slots.
  2. Irradiance (KM stations): the daily irradiance-weighted centroid time minus solar noon in UTC (equation of time, station longitude) is Apr-Sep (local CEST = UTC+2): +4.9 min, Oct-Mar (local CET = UTC+1): +3.9 min. Local time would give ≈ +60 min (winter) / +120 min (summer). The constant ≈ +4 min offset suggests each label is the **end** of its 10-min averaging interval (±10 min is irrelevant for daily aggregates).

## 4. Parsing, flags, completeness

Long table `obs_10min.parquet`: station, ts_utc, variable, sensor_height_cm, value (as published), unit, quality_flag, flag_reason, usable, value_used, tag, raw (non-numeric text). No duplicates (station, ts, variable, height). **Nothing is filled; missing ≠ 0.** Flag rules:

- `invalid` (impossible / sentinel −799.9, ±7999): T ∉ [−30, 50] °C, RH < 0 or > 105 %, wind < 0 or > 75 m/s, precip < 0, irradiance ∉ [0, 1500], pressure ∉ [850, 1080] hPa.
- `suspect` (not used): T ∉ [−20, 42]; RH < 3 % (e.g. C026 7–11 Jun 2018 ≈ 1 % for days = sensor failure); T spike > 6 °C vs both neighbours; T constant ≥ 6 h; RH constant < 99 % ≥ 12 h; wind constant non-zero ≥ 6 h or exactly 0 ≥ 24 h (frozen anemometer, C026 2018/2021).
- `suspect_usable`: **RH in (100, 105] %** (capacitive sensor saturation in fog/rain; 9 % of all RH values, up to 20 % at C077) → used as 100 % in aggregates. Treating them as invalid would delete mostly humid, non-adverse days and inflate adverse frequencies. In the DB they are stored as 100 with flag `suspect` (schema CHECK RH ≤ 100); the raw value stays in the Parquet.
- `missing`: empty/non-numeric element (107,464 values; whole-slot gaps are counted in completeness as expected − present).

## 5. Daily aggregates and the adverse-day indicator

`daily.csv` (station × UTC day 2018–2023, 21,910 rows): Tmax/Tmin/Tmean, RHmin/RHmean (+ time of Tmax / RHmin), max 10-min mean wind, daily mean wind, max gust (Vel.Max), precipitation sum, and n usable slots per variable. **A variable is valid for a day if ≥ 130 of 144 slots (90.3 %) are usable**; a rule is evaluable only if every variable it needs is valid.
- Spatial consistency: a day is excluded if Tmax differs > 8 °C from the median of the other stations (3 station-days) or RHmin < 20 % while the others' median RHmin > 50 % (19 station-days, mostly C0E8 Jan 2018 drifting RH sensor). One of them (C052 2019-07-25, 36 °C / RHmin 19 %) is probably genuine; C052 is not a zone station.
- **Precipitation = interval amounts per 10 min, not accumulated**: 92 % of slots are 0, decreases are as frequent as increases (4.7 % vs 4.5 %; an accumulator would only increase and reset), max 10-min value 18.5 mm, annual sums 810–2327 mm (plausible for Tolosaldea). Daily = sum. Rain day = ≥ 1 mm.

### 5.2 Choice of the primary rule

**ADV-v1 = R4N: Tmax ≥ 25 °C AND RHmin ≤ 40 % AND no rain day in the 3 previous days.** Reasons:
1. **Computable at all 10 stations** with comparable sensors (T/RH screens, tipping-bucket rain gauge); wind exists only at the 4 KM stations and is measured at 9.3–20 m, so a wind threshold is not comparable between stations (C043 at 20 m sees systematically stronger wind).
2. **Non-trivial counts**: 14–23 days at the three zone stations in Jul–Aug 2018–2023 (≈ 2–8 per season), not saturated and not zero.
3. The 3-day antecedent-dry condition is a transparent proxy for dead-fuel drying and removes hot afternoons right after rain; R4 alone (no rain filter) gives 50–85 % more days at the zone stations; R2 (RH ≤ 35) is of similar size to R4N.
4. R1 (with wind ≥ 15 km/h) gives a similar station ranking (Zizurkil lowest, Berastegi ≈ Bidania) — reported as sensitivity, see §7. The wind variant is more fire-relevant in principle, but its station differences are partly instrument height/siting.
This is a demo indicator: thresholds are round numbers, not calibrated against fire records (no fire-occurrence data in the package).

## 7. Zone assignment

Eligible stations: KM stations inside Tolosaldea or ≤ 2 km from it with valid summer data → **C029, C058, C026**. C043 Ordizia (3.4 km outside) is context/sensitivity only; KC/KA river stations are not used (bank-side T/RH at 1–4.35 m, not representative of slopes).
Rule (`assign_method = 'nearest_same_valley'`): for each municipality, representative point = centroid of its analysis-set buildings; elevation = their median B5M ground elevation; station = argmin √(d² + (30·Δz)²), i.e. **1 m of height difference weighs like 30 m of distance** (station elevation = building proxy, §3). Every building inherits its municipality's station (so a building is in exactly one zone). Why: pure straight-line nearest put Tolosa, Alegia and Ikaztegieta (valley floor ≈ 90–110 m) on Bidania (plateau ≈ 520 m); the adjustment sends valley-floor towns to Zizurkil (Oria valley floor, ≈ 70 m) and uplands to Berastegi/Bidania. A factor of 100 was tested and rejected (it pushed Albiztur/Abaltzisketa, next to Bidania, to Berastegi 13 km away). **Limitation**: no DEM in the package, station altitudes are proxies, the factor is a heuristic, and valley/foehn exposure is not modelled.

## 11. Limitations / open issues

- Indicator is exploratory (not FWI, not calibrated with fire records); thresholds are round numbers. Frequency ≠ fire probability.
- Only 4 common seasons; frequencies rest on 13–21 events per zone → large sampling noise (§8).
- Three zones for 28 municipalities; station altitude unknown (proxy from buildings); wind heights differ (9.3–20 m); river-station T/RH not used.
- UTC daily boundary; labels likely end-of-interval (≈ +4 min evidence), not documented by Euskalmet.
- C026 Berastegi: no data Jul 2021–May 2022; C058 Bidania: RH outage Jul–Sep 2018 and invalid T in 2019 (values ≈ −190 °C).
- RH 100–105 % treated as 100 % (documented); RH < 3 % treated as sensor failure.
- Seasonality: July–August is not the peak of adverse days in Tolosaldea (September/October, and foehn in late winter).
